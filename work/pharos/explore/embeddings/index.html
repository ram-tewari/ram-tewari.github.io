Embeddings

Pluggable backends. Vectors from different models are never compared.

● Source audited

PHAROS at 975a38dMIT license

How it works

A chunk is embedded from its signature, docstring, calls and first body lines. The recorded runs here use granite-embedding-english-r2 as an int8 ONNX model on the CPU.

The hash backend exists for offline tests. It is lexical, not semantic, and these recordings do not use it.

Source evidence

  • Pick the embedderbackend/app/codeintel/embeddings.py:475–516Backends are configuration. None means keyword only.
  • Embed the questionbackend/app/codeintel/embeddings.py:341–364ONNX inference on the CPU, with the model's pooling and L2 normalisation.
  • What gets embeddedbackend/app/codeintel/text.py:117–149A chunk is embedded from its signature, docstring, calls and first body lines, not from raw source.
