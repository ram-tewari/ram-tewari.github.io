4 projects
  1. 01

    PHAROS

    The Infinite Catalog / Infrastructure

    A code observatory for AI assistants. Find the function. Follow the evidence.

    AST-aware indexing, source verification at commit, repository-filtered retrieval and native MCP.

    ● Source audited

  2. 02

    Valerie

    The Keeper's Study / Intelligent agents

    A native companion with memory, presence and a deterministic action boundary.

    Typed moves are validated against issued state before native engines execute them.

    ● Source audited

  3. 03

    R&Bot

    The Noir Recording Chamber / Model research

    A 33M-parameter R&B lyrics transformer, trained from scratch. Text generation, not audio generation.

    Trained for four epochs on about 50M tokens, with section tokens for verse, chorus and bridge. Owner-confirmed figures; the source and checkpoint are private.

    ○ Owner-confirmed

  4. 04

    BABEL

    The Theatre of Stories / Creative software

    From written narrative to dramatic structure. An original story, seen another way.

    A Python/React prototype that turns prose into structured visual-scenario blocks.

    ● Source audited
